Site Name

Riverside Roasters

The name identifies the shop's location along the riverfront district and its core product — small-batch roasted coffee. It reads as warm and local rather than corporate, which matches the target audience of neighborhood regulars and remote workers looking for a home base.

Optional domain availability: riverside-roasters.com

Site Purpose

The site gives current and prospective customers everything they need to decide to visit, order ahead, or book the space for a small event:

  • Full menu with prices and dietary/allergen notes.
  • Hours, location, and parking/transit information.
  • A contact and reservation form for private events and large group orders.
  • Loyalty rewards program sign-up.

Scenarios

Representative questions a target-audience visitor would ask, used to drive page content:

  1. What time does Riverside Roasters open on weekends, and do they take reservations for small groups?
  2. Does Riverside Roasters offer oat milk or other dairy-free options, and can I see the full menu with prices online?
  3. How do I sign up for the loyalty rewards program?

Color Schema

This document is styled with the same palette selected for the final site:

  • Espresso Brown #4A3428 — headings, body text, footer background.
  • Warm Cream #F5EFE6 — page background, card backgrounds.
  • Terracotta #C9713D — buttons, links, active nav state, accent borders.
  • Sage Green #6B7C59 — secondary accents, form focus states, small icons.

Typography

Two typefaces keep the page readable while giving headings some character:

  • Poppins (700/600) — used for all headings (h1–h3) and the site logo/wordmark.
  • Source Sans 3 (400/600) — used for body copy, navigation links, and form labels.
